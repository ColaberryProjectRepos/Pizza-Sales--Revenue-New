Executive Summary

This project analyzes historical pizza sales data to identify sales patterns, predict revenue, and support data-driven restaurant decisions. The main challenge was turning large amounts of sales information into clear and actionable business insights.

The project used data cleaning, exploratory analysis, machine learning, and Power BI visualization. Multiple regression models were evaluated for predicting Total Order Revenue, with Gradient Boosting achieving the strongest performance. Model validation and data leakage checks were also performed to improve reliability.

An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to monitor key metrics including Total Revenue, Total Orders, Large Size Revenue, and Average Cost per Order. Sales trends were analyzed by product, pizza size, category, day, and ordering hour.
